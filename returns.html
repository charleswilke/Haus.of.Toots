First, a note...

Haus of Toots canvases are StitchPerfectTM Printed by Needlepaint on either Zweigart or Mono Plus canvas (Zweigart is my first choice when it’s available, and Mono Plus is the alternative that I trust when it isn’t).

I've stitched on both, and I wouldn't put my designs on anything I wasn't happy to stitch myself. Zweigart has been in short supply across the industry, and shortages are expected to continue for the foreseeable future.

I feel strongly about supporting innovation in our industry, but that comes with the responsibility to stand alongside my customers if the stitching experience is impacted.

This policy is that commitment.

In the spirit of transparency

On October 8, 2026, I approved Mono Plus for use when Zweigart is unavailable.

I built up my inventory over the summer, so very few canvases are on Mono Plus, all of which are labeled as of November 15, 2026.

If your canvas arrived before November 15 and you’re not sure which it’s on, reach out and I’ll check.

I design every Haus of Toots canvas to be stitched, so if something about your stitching experience isn’t completely enjoyable, I want to know!

  • Haven't started stitching yet? Let me know within 30 days of delivery and I'll send a replacement or a full refund to your original payment method, prepaid return label included.
  • After 30 days, I'll make it right with a replacement or store credit.
  • Started stitching and hit a problem? Let me know! I'll work with you on a replacement, a refund, or store credit, your call. Yes, that includes partially stitched canvases. I'll send a prepaid label for anything quality-related, so it costs you nothing to send back.
  • Bought from a shop or received as a gift? Reach out! I can't refund what you paid the shop, but I can make it right with a replacement or store credit.
  • Other goods. Needle minders that arrive broken or defective get replaced, no questions.

How to get in touch

Fill out the Make It Right Form with your information and a quick description of what's going on. I read every submission myself and will reply by email within two business days.

If something feels off, reach out!
