About Jessie

Jessie (and Doc) hard at work

Hi, I'm Jessie! I'm originally from Anaheim, California, and now call Omaha, Nebraska home.

I've always loved making things. After years of cross-stitching (and a million other crafts), I found my favorite creative outlet in needlepoint, and a wonderful community along with it.

Haus of Toots is for all stitchers, but especially the ones who are looking for canvases that are a little cheeky and a bit unexpected.
